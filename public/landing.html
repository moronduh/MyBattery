Questions

How is MyBattery different from a regular to-do app?

Most to-do apps treat every day as equal. MyBattery tracks your energy capacity, a number you set each day based on how you feel, and filters your tasks to match. It shows what fits today, not everything you need to do eventually.

It also includes burnout screening, a wellness hub, a focus timer, journaling, and an AI coach.

Does MyBattery work for people with ADHD?

It was designed with variable-energy users in mind, which includes many people with ADHD. There are no streaks, no overwhelming master list, and no shame for low-output days. The focus timer supports shorter, flexible sessions.

Can it help with burnout recovery?

Yes. Onboarding includes a burnout pattern assessment, and the app calibrates task load, pacing, and nudges around your result. You can start at 10% capacity and still have a manageable day.

Is MyBattery free?

You can use MyBattery without an account and without paying. Core features including energy tracking, tasks, the focus timer, journaling, and wellness tools are free.

Is my data private?

Task names and journal entries are encrypted on your device, so we can't read them. We don't sell data or share it with third parties, and you can delete everything from Settings. See the Privacy Policy.

What platforms is it on?

MyBattery works as a web app at mybatteryapp.com on any device with a browser. Native iOS and Android apps are coming soon.
