How it works

Not another to-do list

MyBattery builds your day around your energy — not the other way around.

01 — CHECK IN

Set your battery

Each morning, log how much energy you're actually starting with. MyBattery calibrates everything around that number.

02 — PLAN

Tasks that fit today

Tag tasks by energy cost. The app surfaces only what your current capacity can handle — must, should, or could.

03 — PACE

Move through your day

Check off wins, reschedule what doesn't fit, and get gentle nudges when you're pushing past your limit.

04 — WIND DOWN

Close the day with intention

A two-minute wind-down ritual to carry forward what matters — and let go of what doesn't.

FAQ

Common questions

How is MyBattery different from a regular to-do app?

Most to-do apps treat every day as equal. They let you pile tasks in and offer no guidance on how much is actually manageable. MyBattery tracks your energy capacity — a number you set each day based on how you actually feel — and filters your tasks accordingly. It shows you what fits today, not just everything you need to do eventually.

It also includes burnout screening, a wellness hub, a focus timer, journaling, and an AI coach. It's built around the idea that your energy is the resource to protect, not time.

Does MyBattery work for people with ADHD?

Yes — it was designed with variable-energy users in mind, which includes many people with ADHD. The app doesn't rely on streaks, doesn't show you an overwhelming master list, and doesn't shame you for low-output days. You set your capacity each morning and the app surfaces only what realistically fits. The focus timer also supports working in shorter, flexible sessions rather than demanding rigid hour-long blocks.

Can MyBattery help with burnout recovery?

It's one of the few productivity tools explicitly designed for it. The onboarding includes a burnout pattern assessment, and the app calibrates your task load, pacing, and nudges around your score. You can start at 10% capacity and still have a useful, manageable day. The goal is to protect your energy, not extract it.

Is MyBattery free?

Yes — you can use MyBattery without creating an account and without paying anything. Core features including energy tracking, task management, the focus timer, journaling, and wellness tools are all free. No credit card required to start.

Is my data private?

Your data is encrypted on your device before it's stored. Task names and journal entries are encrypted client-side — we genuinely cannot read them. We don't sell data to advertisers and don't share it with third parties. You can delete everything permanently from Settings at any time. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

What platforms is MyBattery available on?

MyBattery is available as a web app at mybatteryapp.com — it works on any device with a browser, including iPhone, Android, and desktop. No download required.
