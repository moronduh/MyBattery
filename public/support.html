Help with MyBattery — questions, bugs, and account requests

Fastest way to reach us: email info@mybatteryapp.com, or use the Feedback form built into the app (Settings → Control → Feedback). We read every message.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password or change my sign-in email?
Sign out from Settings → Account, then use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. To change your email, contact us at the address above.

How do I delete my data or my account?
Go to Settings → Account. "Clear All Data" permanently deletes your tasks, calendar, and records while keeping your account active. "Delete My Account" permanently removes your account and all associated data and cannot be undone.

How do I manage or cancel my MyBattery Pro subscription?
Go to Settings → Account → Manage, or manage it directly from your device's App Store subscription settings.

Notifications aren't arriving on time.
Check Settings → Notifications to confirm your reminder times and time zone, and make sure notifications are allowed for MyBattery in your device's system settings.

I found a bug or the app crashed.
Please use the in-app Feedback form and select "Bug" — include what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Contact us

Email info@mybatteryapp.com for anything not covered above — bugs, billing questions, feature requests, or general feedback. We typically reply within a few business days.
